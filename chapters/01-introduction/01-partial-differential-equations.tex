\section{Partial Differential Equations}
\label{sec:1-1}

A \emph{partial differential equation} (PDE) is an equation involving an unknown
function of two or more variables and certain of its partial derivatives.

Using the notation explained in Appendix A, we can write out symbolically
a typical PDE, as follows. Fix an integer $k\geq1$ and let $U$ denote an open
subset of $\R^n$.
\par\Needspace{65mm}
\PDEAnnotation{导数数组与对称性}{原书把 $D^ku$ 写成含 $n^k$ 个分量的数组。
若 $u\in C^k$，混合偏导可交换，独立分量只有
$\binom{n+k-1}{k}$ 个。前者按求导次序储存，后者按多重指标计数；两种记号并不矛盾。}

\noindent\textbf{Definition (source, \S1.1).}
An expression of the form
\begingroup
\renewcommand{\theequation}{\arabic{equation}}
\begin{equation}
 F(D^ku(x),D^{k-1}u(x),\ldots,Du(x),u(x),x)=0
 \quad (x\in U)
 \label{eq:source-1-1}
\end{equation}
\endgroup
is called a \emph{$k$th-order partial differential equation}, where
\[
 F:\R^{n^k}\times\R^{n^{k-1}}\times\cdots\times\R^n\times\R\times U\to\R
\]
is given and $u:U\to\R$ is the unknown.

We solve the PDE if we find all $u$ verifying \eqref{eq:source-1-1}, possibly
only among those functions satisfying certain auxiliary boundary conditions
on some part $\Gamma$ of $\partial U$. By finding the solutions we mean,
ideally, obtaining simple, explicit solutions, or, failing that, deducing the
existence and other properties of solutions.

\noindent\textbf{Definitions (source, \S1.1).}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The partial differential equation \eqref{eq:source-1-1} is called
\emph{linear} if it has the form
\[
 \sum_{|\alpha|\leq k}a_\alpha(x)D^\alpha u=f(x)
\]
for given functions $a_\alpha$ $(|\alpha|\leq k)$, $f$. This linear PDE is
\emph{homogeneous} if $f\equiv0$.
\item The PDE \eqref{eq:source-1-1} is \emph{semilinear} if it has the form
\[
 \sum_{|\alpha|=k}a_\alpha(x)D^\alpha u
 +a_0(D^{k-1}u,\ldots,Du,u,x)=0.
\]
\item The PDE \eqref{eq:source-1-1} is \emph{quasilinear} if it has the form
\begin{align*}
 &\sum_{|\alpha|=k}a_\alpha(D^{k-1}u,\ldots,Du,u,x)D^\alpha u\\
 &\hspace{24mm}+a_0(D^{k-1}u,\ldots,Du,u,x)=0.
\end{align*}
\item The PDE \eqref{eq:source-1-1} is \emph{fully nonlinear} if it depends
nonlinearly upon the highest order derivatives.
\end{enumerate}
\par\Needspace{65mm}
\PDEAnnotation{分类看最高阶导数}{先找阶数 $k$，再看 $D^ku$ 如何出现。
半线性：最高阶系数只依赖 $x$；拟线性：系数可依赖低阶导数。
例如 $u_t+uu_x=0$ 是一阶拟线性，而 $u_t+uu_x+u_{xxx}=0$ 是三阶半线性。}

A \emph{system of partial differential equations} is, informally speaking,
a collection of several PDE for several unknown functions.

\noindent\textbf{Definition (source, \S1.1).}
Let $k$ be a positive integer. An expression of the form
\begingroup
\renewcommand{\theequation}{\arabic{equation}}
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf F(D^k\mathbf u(x),\ldots,D\mathbf u(x),\mathbf u(x),x)=\mathbf0
 \quad(x\in U)
 \label{eq:source-1-2}
\end{equation}
\endgroup
is called a \emph{$k$th-order system of partial differential equations}, where
\[
 \mathbf F:\R^{mn^k}\times\R^{mn^{k-1}}\times\cdots
 \times\R^{mn}\times\R^m\times U\to\R^m
\]
is given and
\[
 \mathbf u:U\to\R^m,\qquad \mathbf u=(u^1,\ldots,u^m)
\]
is the unknown.

Here we are supposing that the system comprises the same number $m$ of
scalar equations as unknowns $(u^1,\ldots,u^m)$. This is the most common
circumstance, although other systems may have fewer or more equations than
unknowns. Systems are classified in the obvious way as being linear,
semilinear, etc.

\noindent\textbf{Notation.}
We write ``PDE'' as an abbreviation for both the singular ``partial
differential equation'' and the plural ``partial differential equations''.

\subsection*{Editorial supplement: the multiindex convention}
For $\alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}^n$, set
\[
 |\alpha|=\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i,\qquad
 D^\alpha u=\partial_{x_1}^{\alpha_1}\cdots\partial_{x_n}^{\alpha_n}u.
\]
The \emph{principal part} of a linear operator of order $k$ is
$\sum_{|\alpha|=k}a_\alpha(x)D^\alpha$; the terms of smaller order are its
lower-order terms. Here $D^0u=u$. For example, when $n=2$,
$D^{(2,1)}u=u_{x_1x_1x_2}$ and $|(2,1)|=3$.

The words linear, semilinear and quasilinear describe nested forms.
When a single classification is requested, we report the most restrictive
applicable form: linear first, then semilinear, then quasilinear.
These definitions concern the displayed differential operator. An algebraic
rewriting that changes its form need not preserve its classification.
\par\Needspace{65mm}
\PDEAnnotation{等价零点不保结构}{把 $Lu=0$ 改写成 $(Lu)^3=0$，经典解集不变，
但新表达式对最高阶导数非线性，且在线性化时可能退化。
因此分类必须说明采用哪个算子表达式，不能只看方程的解集。}
