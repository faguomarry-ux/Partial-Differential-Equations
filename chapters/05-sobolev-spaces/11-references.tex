\section{References}
\label{sec:5-11}

The primary source is Lawrence C. Evans, \emph{Partial Differential
Equations}, second edition, Chapter 5, printed pp.~239--292
(local English PDF pp.~256--309). The chapter order and source theorem
identifiers follow that edition. The Chinese scan has Chapter 5 at
printed pp.~169--207 (PDF pp.~187--225); the chapter opening and its
list of section starts were visually checked. It is a terminology
reference, not the source of the English prose written here.

For Sobolev theory, Evans's \S5.11 points to
Gilbarg--Trudinger (Chapter 7), Lieb--Loss, Ziemer, and Evans--Gariepy.
For BMO the source points to Stein, Chapter IV; for time-dependent
spaces to Temam, pp.~248--273; and for whole-space Hardy to Tartar,
Chapter 17. These are source pointers, not claims that those books
have been independently consulted for this draft.
