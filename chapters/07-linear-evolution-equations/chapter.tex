\medskip
We pass from elliptic boundary problems to trajectories in function spaces.
Parabolic equations dissipate energy; hyperbolic equations transport it.
Galerkin approximation treats time-dependent coefficients, Fourier analysis
detects matrix structure, and semigroups organize autonomous evolution through
elliptic resolvents. Constants may depend on the domain, final time,
ellipticity, and stated coefficient norms, never on the approximation index.
