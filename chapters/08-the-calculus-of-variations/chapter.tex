\medskip
The central question is how an energy produces a PDE. First variations give
the equation; coercivity and lower semicontinuity produce minimizers;
constraints replace equations by multipliers or variational inequalities.
Minimax methods locate further critical points, while invariance yields
conservation and monotonicity laws. Unless stated otherwise, $U$ is bounded
with smooth boundary and $1<q<\infty$. A prescribed trace is represented
by a fixed Sobolev lift, and all existence claims below require a finite-energy
competitor. External regularity and divergence-solving theorems are identified
where used.
