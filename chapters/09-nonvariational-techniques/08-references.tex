\section{References}
\label{sec:9-8}
\subsection*{Source directions}
Evans's \S9.8 points to J.-L. Lions [L2] and Zeidler [Zd, Vol.~2] for
monotonicity, and to [E1] for nonlinear weak-convergence methods.
For fixed points the source recommends Zeidler [Zd, Vol.~1] and
Gilbarg--Trudinger [G-T, Chapter~11]; for ordered methods, Smoller
[S, Chapter~10]. The blow-up discussion follows Payne [Pa, \S9]; the
small-data theorem is attributed there to H.~Fujita, J.~Fac.~Sci.~U.~Tokyo
Sect.~I 13 (1966), 109--124. The radial symmetry result is attributed to
Gidas, Ni and Nirenberg, Comm.~Math.~Phys.~68 (1979), 209--243.
The convex-flow material follows Brezis [Br2]; Barbu [Ba] and Zeidler
[Zd, Vol.~2] are further directions for nonlinear semigroups.
These are the source's attributions and bibliography keys, not a claim
that all of those works have been independently consulted for this draft.

\subsection*{Proof dependencies and scope of this reconstruction}
The finite-dimensional input is Brouwer from \S8.1. Functional analysis
inputs include weak compactness of bounded Hilbert-space sequences,
Hahn--Banach separation, Sobolev and Rellich embeddings, and the
Hilbert-space Picard theorem. PDE inputs are the linear elliptic and
parabolic theories of Chapters 6--7, Sobolev truncation and time chain
rules from Chapter 5, and convex integral lower semicontinuity from
Chapter 8. The global boundary $H^2$ step in \S9.1 explicitly uses the
boundary difference-quotient argument of Chapters 6 and 8.

Editorial changes include a bounded-Jacobian hypothesis for that $H^2$
claim, an $L^2$ restart formulation for reaction--diffusion, a complete
compact-convex-hull argument in Schaefer, extremal ordered solutions,
a quantified small-support proof of moving-plane continuation, and a
space--time resolvent test identifying the gradient-flow limit.
The critical Fujita endpoint, general critical sign-changing Pohozaev
nonexistence, arbitrary-data $H^2$ bounds down to time zero, general
BV contraction, and reconstruction of a sandpile flux are not proved.
English exposition, proofs, Chinese mathematical annotations, solutions,
and TikZ diagrams are editorial reconstructions or additions.
Compilation and explicit model checks do not constitute independent
mathematical review.
