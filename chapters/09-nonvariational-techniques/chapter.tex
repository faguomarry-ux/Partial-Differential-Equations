\noindent This editorial study chapter reconstructs Evans's arguments in English,
with Chinese mathematical annotations and additional proof steps. Source theorem
numbers are separate from the continuous numbering of these notes. Monotonicity,
compactness, order, scaling, and convex dissipation provide different mechanisms.
Unless specified otherwise, $U\subset\R^n$ is bounded with smooth boundary,
$H^1_0(U)$ has norm $\|Du\|_2$, and $C_P$ is its Poincar\'e constant.
This study draft has not undergone independent mathematical review.
