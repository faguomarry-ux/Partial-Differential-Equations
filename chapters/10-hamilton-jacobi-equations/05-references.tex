\section{References}
\label{sec:10-5}
\subsection*{Source attributions}
Evans's \S10.5 attributes the test-function formulation to Crandall,
Evans and Lions, Trans.~AMS 282 (1984), 487--502, following the
foundational Crandall--Lions paper, Trans.~AMS 277 (1983), 1--42.
The uniqueness argument follows the first paper, with improvements
acknowledged there to M.~Crandall. The control connection is attributed
to P.-L.~Lions [Li]. Further directions in the source are Tran [Tn],
Fleming--Soner [F-S], and Bardi--Capuzzo Dolcetta [B-CD]. These are
source attributions and bibliography keys. The source credits D.~Ostrov
with Problem 8 and directs readers to Cabr\'e--Caffarelli [C-C] and
Gilbarg--Trudinger [G-T] for the fully nonlinear elliptic exercises.
The present reconstruction
does not claim separate consultation of all those works.

\subsection*{Dependencies and proof boundaries}
The proofs use compactness on contact balls, mollification, elementary
convex duality from Chapter 3, Rademacher's theorem, the integral form
of the ODE and Gronwall. The comparison proof is first-order: freezing
the other variables produces smooth tests directly. No second-order
matrix jet lemma is being assumed or proved. The source's uniqueness
theorem is expanded to comparison of BUC sub- and supersolutions.
An additional comparison theorem with one spatially Lipschitz competitor
explicitly supplies the superlinear Hopf--Lax uniqueness step.

The vanishing-viscosity argument is conditional on smooth approximants
and locally uniform convergence; existence and uniform estimates for a
general nonconvex Hamiltonian are not proved here. Value-function existence
is proved for the stated bounded continuous coefficients and compact
control set; its terminal Hamiltonian is concave, and the time-reversed
initial Hamiltonian is convex. Smooth verification is conditional on an
admissible minimizing trajectory. General nonsmooth feedback synthesis,
existence of an optimal ordinary control under additional hypotheses,
second-order comparison, Perron's method, and nonlocal or state-constrained
boundary theories are outside this chapter's completed proofs.
The 14 problem solutions and all diagrams are editorial additions.
Source checking, symbolic model checking, compilation, and visual QA are
recorded separately; none is an independent mathematical review.
