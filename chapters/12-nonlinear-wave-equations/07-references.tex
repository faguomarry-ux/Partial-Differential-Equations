\subsection*{Proof boundaries and editorial changes}
The chapter uses the earlier linear wave, Sobolev, Hardy, Rellich,
Gronwall, Banach fixed-point, Fourier, and measure-theoretic tools.
The product exponent allocation and completeness of the strong ball
in the weak metric are supplied. Sign-condition weak existence uses
space-time uniform integrability; its energy inequality is distinguished
from smooth conservation and supplies only finite-strip $L^2$ bounds.
For noninteger powers, global bounded finite-energy mild solutions
are asserted instead of unqualified smoothness. Sphere-valued maps
are interpreted as constant outside a compact set, not zero there.

The homogeneous $(4,12)$ three-dimensional Strichartz estimate is an
explicit external theorem. Its dispersive proof is not supplied;
the energy and Duhamel parts, localization by cone-supported forcing,
Morawetz identity, singular-top limit, nonconcentration, cone enlargement,
finite cover, and continuation are expanded. No solution at the candidate
singular time is assumed in advance. The critical proof does not claim
a general supercritical theorem or scattering. The focusing small-data
proof covers $1<p<1+\sqrt2$, not the critical endpoint or the range above
it. The NLS virial argument assumes finite variance and enough decay.
All 18 solutions, Chinese notes, and five diagrams are editorial
reconstructions. Source comparison, symbolic checks, compilation,
and visual review are separate from independent mathematical review,
which has not been performed.
