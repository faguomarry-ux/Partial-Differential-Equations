\section*{English text and margin notes}
Read the main argument first. Margin notes will explain selected vocabulary,
long sentences, and mathematical ideas. They will be short enough to remain
beside the relevant passage. Longer derivations belong in the main text.
\PDEVocab{provided that}{意为“只要”或“在……条件下”。阅读定理时，应把这类表达后面的内容识别为假设。}

\section*{Expanded proofs}
An expanded proof will state the assumptions, explain the strategy, and fill
in omitted calculations. Each use of approximation, integration by parts,
compactness, or passage to a limit must identify the applicable hypotheses.
Source theorem numbers will be recorded explicitly; the notes' own numbering
does not automatically coincide with the original book.

\section*{Current status}
The two source prefaces have been typeset in English with selected Chinese
margin notes. Chapters~1--12 and Appendices~A--E remain outlines: their
exposition, proofs, and exercises have not yet been transcribed or expanded.
The margin notes on this page demonstrate the annotation system.
